# GEV-UZ Decisions

## D-001 — Preserve upstream compatibility

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-15

GEV-UZ will prefer configuration, additive modules and narrow extension points over broad rewrites of upstream code. This reduces recurring merge conflicts and keeps security/performance improvements from upstream easier to adopt.

## D-002 — Tashkent first, global capabilities retained

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-15

The first regional experience is Tashkent/Uzbekistan, but existing global capabilities will remain available unless there is a specific technical, legal or licensing reason to disable a source.

## D-003 — Three product languages

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-15

The product localization target is Uzbek Latin, Russian and English. Localization must use a reusable i18n architecture rather than one-off string replacements.

## D-004 — No secret/private surveillance feeds

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-15

GEV-UZ will integrate public/open or explicitly authorized data sources. It will not market historical basemap imagery as live satellite imagery, and it will keep source provenance visible.

## D-005 — Attribution and data licensing are first-class constraints

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-15

The upstream MIT licence applies to source code, not automatically to all bundled/runtime datasets and models. Commercial deployment requires a provider-by-provider licence audit.
